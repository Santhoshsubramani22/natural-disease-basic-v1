Supported Disaster Types

Flood

Evaluates 24h/7d rainfall, river levels, soil saturation, and elevation.

Earthquake

Analyzes magnitude, depth, fault line proximity, and ground acceleration.

Cyclone

Monitors wind gust speeds, central pressure, and storm surge levels.

Wildfire

Evaluates vegetation dryness, fire weather index, and temperature anomalies.

Landslide

Calculates slope degree, terrain roughness, and soil saturation.

Drought

Assesses precipitation anomalies, drought indices, and groundwater levels.

Tsunami

Evaluates undersea seismic magnitude, fault distance, and coastal sea level anomalies.
